# PT-v6 update

This remains a **standalone Patient Trainer only**. It is not merged into Medical Dictionary.

## Examination
- posterior lung auscultation/percussion targets moved upward onto the thorax
- sacral oedema target moved upward onto the sacral region
- added bilateral renal-angle tapotement (CVA percussion) under Palpate
- Examination workspace hides the unrelated left patient rail and right coaching/checklist rail
- removed the redundant Current finding card; one findings log remains, newest result first
- auscultation sounds can be replayed from the findings log

## Investigations
- 12-lead ECG is locked until a 10-electrode placement game is completed
- RA, LA, RL, LL + V1-V6 must be positioned correctly on Peter
- click-to-place and drag/drop are both supported
- Practice mode gives placement hints after an error; Exam mode only reports incorrect placement
- after all 10 are correct the ECG is acquired and the case tracing opens
