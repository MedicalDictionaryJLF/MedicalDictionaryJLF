# Avatar v3 – Head and trunk refinement

This revision focuses on improving the visible quality of the examination avatar after hotspot placement was largely corrected in v2.

## Improved areas
- more realistic head shape with a softer jawline and clearer cheeks
- improved hairline and cranial contour
- less awkward neck transition
- cleaner face proportions (eyes, brows, nose, mouth, ears)
- broader, more adult-looking upper thorax
- better clavicular/shoulder transition
- more natural chest-to-waist silhouette
- updated posterior trunk contours

## Minor coordinate touch-ups
Small position refinements were applied to neck, cardiac, lung and abdominal points so they continue to sit correctly on the improved body geometry.
