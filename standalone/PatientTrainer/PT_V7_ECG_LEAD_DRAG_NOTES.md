# PT-v7 ECG lead dragging update

## Added in this version
- coloured draggable ECG lead illustrations instead of plain text chips
- drag-and-drop still supported, with click-to-select fallback
- coloured placed markers on the patient image
- explicit feedback for misplaced chest leads
- explicit feedback for limb-lead colour mismatches (e.g. white RA dragged to LA position)
- retry reminder when the lead is very misplaced or the limb colour/side does not match

## Limb lead colours used
- RA = red
- LA = yellow
- RL = green
- LL = black

## Notes
The rest of PT-v6 remains unchanged. This build is still standalone only and not merged into Medical Dictionary.
