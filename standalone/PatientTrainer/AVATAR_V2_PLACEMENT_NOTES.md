# Interactive Physical Examination – Avatar v2

This revision focuses only on the physical-examination SVG and hotspot placement.

## What changed
- Rebuilt the examination avatar with more realistic adult proportions:
  - smaller head relative to torso
  - longer neck
  - broader shoulders and more natural chest/abdomen proportions
  - hands, wrists, knees, ankles and feet moved to anatomically coherent locations
  - anterior landmarks: clavicular/pectoral contours, sternum, costal margin, umbilicus
  - posterior landmarks: scapulae, spine and lumbar contours
- Preserved breathing, blinking, pallor and diaphoresis overlays.
- Reduced hotspot visual size slightly so closely spaced cardiac sites are easier to distinguish.

## Major hotspot corrections
- Lips moved from the eye/nasal level to the actual mouth.
- JVP and carotid/tracheal sites moved onto the neck.
- Left/right hands now respect patient anatomical laterality in the anterior view.
- Radial pulse / hand sites moved from the forearm to the distal wrist/hand.
- Cardiac areas retuned around the sternal borders and left 5th intercostal/apical region.
- Anterior and posterior lung fields redistributed over the thorax.
- Abdominal quadrants moved out of the shorts/upper thighs and onto the abdomen.
- Calf and ankle points moved laterally onto the actual lower limbs.

## Orientation rule
On the anterior view, the patient's RIGHT is on the viewer's LEFT, and the patient's LEFT is on the viewer's RIGHT.
