# Clinical Patient Trainer: asset provenance

The new patients use the MakeHuman / MPFB anatomical mesh, identity deformation
targets, skin maps, hair, eyes, lashes, brows, teeth, tongue and garment assets.
These core graphical assets are released under **CC0 1.0** by the MakeHuman
Community. Adaptations include patient identities, fitted ward clothing, skin
materials, skeletal poses, clinical gestures and facial animation clips.

- Official asset license: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/about/license.html
- MPFB source and code license: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2
- Core assets: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html
- Facial units: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/faceunits01.html
- Visemes: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/visemes02.html

MPFB is a development tool distributed under GPL. The graphical output is covered
by the separate CC0 asset license. The build scripts and editable Blender files
are supplied under `tools/character-pipeline/` for maintenance.

The three hospital room sets are newly authored polygonal environments for this
release. Their geometry and materials are supplied with the project.

Three.js r180 is MIT licensed; its license is included at
`assets/vendor/three/LICENSE.txt` in the project root.

The old Mike Alger patient and Yvo Pors bed assets, and the supplied procedural
Peter model, are not used by the new clinical renderer. The original repository's
legacy assets and their original attribution files are retained.

Appearances are fictional visual interpretations. Age, sex and clinical evidence
follow the source cases. Undocumented height, specific facial features and hair
are cosmetic choices; they are not added to the clinical records. The characters
and clinical motions have not been independently medically validated.
