This pack contains backbone CSV charts for Course 1 of medical Latin.

Files:
- lesson1_theory_backbone.csv
- lesson1_pronunciation_backbone.csv
- lesson1_declension_I_paradigms.csv
- lesson1_noun_plus_noun_paradigm.csv
- lesson1_noun_plus_adjective_paradigm.csv
- lesson1_preposition_paradigm.csv
- course_backbone_overview.csv

The files are designed as didactic templates and paradigm charts, not as vocabulary databases.
You can now replace or expand the examples with your own word lists.
